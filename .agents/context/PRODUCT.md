# SportsBuilder
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Nontechnical coaches and athletes inspecting movement and building reusable coaching tools.
## Product Purpose
Upload, inspect, and understand sports footage; interview a builder to configure reliable metric and coaching primitives.
## Capabilities and Constraints
See [specification](../../docs/SPEC.md). Local prototype, React/TypeScript, Python/FastAPI, Neon, Daytona. Two screens, no auth, no deployment, no tool editing after creation. Body tracking first. JSON config and deterministic math; AI provides configuration and coaching, never numerical evidence.
## Brand Commitments
SportsBuilder name. Inherit LearnUnlock's Clear Path Studio identity, components, Lexend, cool neutrals, rare teal, compact type, restrained surfaces. Minimalism is the user's explicit preference.
## Evidence on Hand
User supplied sketch references and product requirements. No real sports footage supplied yet. Synthetic examples must be labeled.
